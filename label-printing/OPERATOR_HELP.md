## How to print

1. Use Chrome or Edge on the computer connected to the Brother QL-600. Turn on the printer and load DK-1204 labels.
2. Review the drinks and quantities. Uncheck drinks you do not want to print, or use Select everything. Each drink variation includes one backup label.
3. Click Print Selected to preview every selected label. Scroll to check drink names, machine slots, and expiration dates. Uncheck individual labels here to print only one as a test.
4. Click Print Labels to confirm. Choose the QL-600 in the browser permission window if asked. The printer cuts between drink variations.

The browser remembers permission for this site and tries to reconnect on later visits. Connect QL-600 also lets you choose the printer before opening the preview.

## Troubleshooting

- Printer missing? Check its power and USB cable, use Chrome or Edge, and click Connect QL-600 again.
- Cannot connect? Close other apps or tabs using the printer, reconnect the USB cable, and try again.
- Plan missing or out of date? Check your connection, then click Reload. Reload selects all drinks again and recalculates expiration as today's UTC date plus seven days.
- Printing stopped? Check the label roll and Status Log. Check which labels printed before reopening the preview and retrying to avoid duplicates.
